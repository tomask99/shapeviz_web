THE PERSON
BEHIND SHAPEVIZ.

I’m Tomáš, and I’ve been working in 3D graphics for over ten years. I collaborate with interior designers and furniture manufacturers. I’ve also worked with several international CGI studios on larger commercial and advertising projects, including work for brands such as Prada and Hugo Boss.

I studied building construction and architecture, which gives me an understanding of the technical aspects of the buildings and interiors I work with. I enjoy bringing the real world into 3D and contributing to creative CGI productions.
